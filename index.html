About Our School

A Trusted Institution Since 1998

Baba Deep Singh ji Shaheed Public School, Chawinda Devi (Near Kathunangal) Amritsar is a trusted institution dedicated to providing quality education and strong moral values to young learners.

Established in 1998 under the leadership of President S. Ravinder Pal Singh, the school was founded with the vision of creating a nurturing environment where students can grow academically, socially and ethically.

Messages from Our Leadership

S. Ravinder Pal Singh

President

Education is the most powerful tool that can transform a society and shape the future of a nation. When we established Baba Deep Singh ji Shaheed Public School in 1998, our dream was simple yet profound — to create a place where every child receives not only academic knowledge but also strong moral values, discipline, and character.

Over the years, we have remained committed to providing a safe, nurturing, and inspiring environment where students can grow holistically. We believe that true education goes beyond textbooks. It builds confidence, compassion, and a sense of responsibility towards society.

Mrs. Baljit Kaur Khalsa

Principal

Every child is unique and carries immense potential within them. At Baba Deep Singh ji Shaheed Public School, our dedicated teachers work tirelessly to create a joyful, safe, and inspiring learning environment where students feel valued and motivated to explore their abilities.

We focus not only on academic excellence but also on character building, emotional strength, and life skills. Our aim is to prepare students so that they can face the challenges of life with courage, integrity, kindness, and confidence.

Our History

Baba Deep Singh ji Shaheed Public School, Chawinda Devi (Near Kathunangal) Amritsar is a trusted institution dedicated to providing quality education and strong moral values to young learners. Established in 1998 under the leadership of President S. Ravinder Pal Singh, the school was founded with the vision of creating a nurturing environment where students can grow academically, socially and ethically.

We are associated with PSEB (Chandigarh) Mohali and offer classes from Nursery to 10th standard.

Academics

Baba Deep Singh ji Shaheed Public School offers a well-structured academic program from Nursery to Class 10th, affiliated with PSEB (Chandigarh) Mohali.

Nursery – UKG

Play-based learning, language development, motor skills, and basic concepts in a joyful environment.

Class 1 – 5

Strong foundation in English, Mathematics, EVS, and moral education with activity-based teaching.

Class 6 – 10

PSEB curriculum with focus on Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, and languages. Regular assessments and board exam guidance.
